# Viral CTA Rewriter

Rewrite CTA theo **Rule 4 (Non-Values-Based)** + **Rule 5 (Non-Autocratic)** trong Communication Algorithm. Chuyển CTA từ dạng ra lệnh thành dạng mời gọi.

## Tại sao CTA truyền thống fail

**85% dân số** không thích bị ra lệnh. Autocratic CTA như "Buy now!" chỉ hit 5% dân (Action-based). Values-based CTA như "Our product is the best" chỉ hit 10% dân (Opinion-based). Tổng cộng có thể đang làm bạn mất **75-90% audience**.

## 3 style thay thế — reach 85%

| Style | % dân | Đặc điểm | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Democratic** | 35% | Đặt câu hỏi, mời participation | "Do you want to...?" |
| **Benevolent** | 30% | Warm, caring, nhẹ nhàng | "We'd love to show you..." |
| **Laissez-faire** | 20% | Vui, casual, slang | "Gotta sign up to show up!" |

**Tổng:** 85% dân → 3x reach vs Autocratic thuần.

## Red flags cần fix ngay

### Autocratic keywords (ra lệnh)
- "Buy now", "Order now", "Click here", "Sign up now"
- "Act fast", "Hurry", "Don't miss out", "Last chance"
- "Subscribe", "Download", "Register today"
- "Get your copy", "Claim yours"
- Bất kỳ imperative verb đứng đầu câu

### Values-based keywords (áp giá trị/ý kiến)
- "should", "must", "need to", "have to", "ought to"
- "the best", "#1", "only the...", "nothing better than"
- "We believe...", "In our opinion..."
- "True [X] know that..."
- "Real [profession]/people do..."

## Conversion table (dùng trực tiếp)

| Autocratic ban đầu | Benevolent | Democratic | Laissez-faire |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Buy now or miss out on this incredible offer!" | "We have an offer for you." | "Do you want to check out our best offer yet?" | "Our offers are the bomb right now!" |
| "Act fast and secure your spot!" | "We want you to secure your spot." | "Would you like to take advantage of our limited-time offer?" | "Spots are disappearing like MySpace." |
| "Sign up now!" | "We would love it if you signed up now." | "Do you want to sign up?" | "Gotta sign up to show up!" |
| "Subscribe to our newsletter or miss out!" | "Our newsletter is created to help you." | "Do you want to stay updated?" | "Click! Sign! Bang! You got the newsletter." |
| "Get our product now!" | "We have a new experience just for you!" | "Do you want our new product?" | "New experience! New product... what, what!!!" |
| "Upgrade to our premier package!" | "We want you to sign up for our premier package." | "Do you want to unlock our top-tier resources?" | "Upgrading is like driving your dream car..." |
| "Don't think twice; click here!" | "We dropped the prices just for YOU." | "Do you want to save money this weekend?" | "Dropping that price for a sizzling weekend!" |

| Values-based ban đầu | Feelings | Facts | Fun |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Our supplement is the best" | "We have a supplement just for you" | "Per X research, we have more vitamins than competitors" | "More nutrients — just what the doc ordered!" |
| "You should meditate" | "Quiet, soothing meditation may help you" | "Research shows meditation benefits 82% of people" | "Meditation, dude — might help ya out!" |
| "To be a good creator, you must work with Hook Point" | "Hook Point would love to help you go viral" | "Hook Point has helped X creators go viral — here's the proof" | "Wanna get your viral content exploding? Hook Point!" |

## Workflow

### 1. Inventory tất cả CTA
Quét: landing page headline, subhead, button text, bio, caption cuối post, email footer, ads creative.

### 2. Tag từng CTA
[A] Autocratic / [V] Values-based / [OK] pass

### 3. Rewrite với 3 option mỗi CTA
Cho mỗi A/V, đưa Benevolent + Democratic + Laissez-faire (hoặc Feelings + Facts + Fun nếu là Values-based).

### 4. Chọn style phù hợp audience
- B2B serious → Benevolent hoặc Facts
- Gen Z / Lifestyle → Laissez-faire hoặc Fun
- Mass consumer → Democratic

### 5. Thêm câu hỏi thay tuyên bố
Kỹ thuật mạnh: biến tuyên bố thành câu hỏi mời tương tác.
- ❌ "Our course teaches financial freedom"
- ✅ "Do you want to see how our students reached financial freedom?"

## Output format

```markdown
# CTA AUDIT — [Brand / Page URL]

## Inventory & Tag
| Location | Current CTA | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Landing headline | "Transform Your Life Today!" | A |
| CTA button | "Sign Up Now" | A |
| Bio | "The #1 finance coach in VN" | V |
| Post caption #1 | "You should start saving today" | V |

## Rewrite

### Landing headline
- **B:** "We'd love to help you transform your life"
- **D:** "Do you want to see how our students transformed their lives?"
- **L:** "Life transformation, here we go!"
- **Recommended:** Democratic (invite curiosity)

### CTA button
- **B:** "Join us"
- **D:** "Ready to start?"
- **L:** "Let's go!"
- **Recommended:** Democratic

[repeat for all]

## Projected reach
Before: ~15% dân (A + V heavy)
After: ~85% dân (B + D + L mix)
```

## Examples for house listing (case study trong sách)
Thay vì listing facts khô khan, blend 5 style:
> 4BR/3BA, 2,300 sqft, [price] (**Facts**)
> We'd love to show this comfy cozy home (**Benevolent**)
> A home with warmth you never want to leave (**Feelings**)
> There's a pool where you can host amazing parties! (**Fun**)
> Do you want to live near top schools? (**Democratic**)
> This hip spot won't last long... let me know when you wanna check it out (**Laissez-faire**)

## Anti-patterns
- ❌ Đổi "Buy now" thành "Please buy now please" — vẫn Autocratic
- ❌ Đổi "Must-have product" thành "Really must-have" — vẫn Values-based
- ❌ Dùng cả 3 style dồn dập — phản tác dụng, chọn 1-2 style phù hợp context
